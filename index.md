# Privacy Policy — Patchwork Bug Notebook

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

Patchwork is a notebook for recording software issues, notes and to-do lists. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## Summary

**Patchwork does not collect, transmit or share any of your data.** Everything you write stays on your device.

## Information we collect

None. Patchwork has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The developer cannot see the issues, notes or lists you create.

## Where your data is stored

Issues, notes and to-do lists are saved as files inside the app's private storage on your iPhone or iPad. The app makes no network requests and works fully offline.

Because this storage is part of the app's data, it may be included in your own iPhone or iPad backups (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup) according to your device settings. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by the developer.

## Sharing

Your data is never sold, shared or sent to the developer or any third party.

## Deleting your data

Delete an individual issue or note from inside the app. Deleting the Patchwork app from your device removes all of its stored data.

## Children

Patchwork does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page with a new date.

# Gizlilik Politikası — Patchwork

_Son güncelleme: 5 Ekim 2026_

Patchwork, yazılım hatalarını, notları ve yapılacaklar listelerini kaydetmek için bir defterdir.

**Patchwork hiçbir veriyi toplamaz, göndermez veya paylaşmaz.** Oluşturduğunuz issue, not ve listeler yalnızca cihazınızdaki uygulamaya ait özel alanda saklanır. Uygulamada hesap, giriş, analiz, reklam veya üçüncü taraf SDK'sı yoktur ve uygulama ağ isteği yapmaz; tamamen çevrimdışı çalışır.

Uygulama verileri, cihaz ayarlarınıza göre kendi iPhone/iPad yedeklerinize (ör. iCloud Yedekleme) dahil olabilir. Bu yedekler sizin ve Apple'ın kontrolündedir.

Tek tek kayıtları uygulama içinden silebilirsiniz. Uygulamayı cihazdan sildiğinizde tüm verileri de silinir.
